# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Disagree about code without making it
about the person.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to contributors regardless of experience, background, or
  identity.
- Give feedback on the work, not the author.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution, and that "no" is a
  legitimate answer that does not require a debate.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others'
private information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Enforcement

Report problems through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Spriz/meeting-blaster/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the maintainer through GitHub.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete comments and may block accounts for
behaviour they judge inappropriate. Reports will be handled discreetly.
